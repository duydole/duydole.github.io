Common questions

Does Lullo cost anything?
No. Lullo is completely free — every sound, timer and mix. There are no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Will sound keep playing with the screen locked?
Yes. Start a sound, then lock your iPhone. You can pause or resume it from the Lock Screen or Control Center.

How does the sleep timer work?
Tap the timer button and pick 15 minutes to 2 hours, or "Until I stop". When the timer ends, the sound fades out gently over 30 seconds.

How do I save a mix?
Layer up to three sounds on the Sounds tab, then tap the heart. Your saved mixes appear on the Mixes tab; swipe left on one to delete it.

Does Lullo need the internet?
No. Every sound is generated live on your iPhone, so it works offline.

Safe listening
Keep your phone at least 2 m (7 ft) from the crib and the volume low — about as loud as a quiet shower.
