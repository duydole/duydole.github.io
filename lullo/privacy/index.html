Privacy Policy

Lullo: Baby Sleep Sounds · Effective October 18, 2026

In short: Lullo does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Information we collect

None. Lullo does not collect any personal information or usage data, and it does not send anything about you or your device to us or to third parties.

Information stored on your device

To work, Lullo keeps a few things only on your iPhone:

  • Mixes you save (their names, sounds and volumes)
  • Your playback settings, such as the last sounds you played, your sleep-timer choice and the slow fade-in option
  • Whether you have finished the introduction screens, and a simple counter used to decide when to show Apple's rating prompt

This data never leaves your device through Lullo. It is removed when you delete the app, and you can delete your saved mixes any time in Settings → Delete All Saved Mixes. If you use iCloud or iTunes backups, Apple may include this data in your device backup under Apple's own privacy policy.

Sounds and internet access

All sounds are generated on your device. Lullo does not need an internet connection and does not access the microphone, camera, contacts, photos or location.

Third-party services

Lullo has no third-party SDKs, advertising or analytics. The rating prompt is shown by Apple (StoreKit), and links such as "Contact Support" open Apple's Mail app or Safari, which are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Lullo is made for parents and caregivers. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
