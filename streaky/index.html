Common questions

How do I log a habit?
Tap the round check button on the habit's card. For habits with a daily goal (for example 8 glasses of water), each tap adds one. Long-press the button to undo.

I forgot to log yesterday. Can I fix it?
Yes. Open the habit and tap any past day on the monthly calendar to toggle it.

When does a streak break?
Today stays open until midnight, so your streak only breaks if a whole day passes without reaching the goal.

How do I add a widget?
Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap +, search for Streaky and pick a size. Touch and hold the small widget and choose Edit Widget to pick which habit it shows. You can check off habits right from the widget.

The widget doesn't show my latest change.
Widgets normally refresh within a few seconds. If one looks out of date, open Streaky once; that refreshes every widget.

Reminders don't appear.
Make sure notifications are allowed for Streaky in the iPhone Settings → Notifications → Streaky, and that the reminder is turned on for that day in the habit editor.

What does Streaky Pro include?
The free plan includes up to 3 habits and the small widget. Pro unlocks unlimited habits, medium and large widgets, reminders, archiving and CSV export. You can choose yearly (with a 3-day free trial) or weekly.

How do I restore or cancel my subscription?
Tap Restore Purchases in Streaky's Settings. To cancel, open the iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Streaky. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will my habits sync to my other devices?
Not yet. Your habits are stored only on this iPhone and are included in your regular iCloud or computer backup.
