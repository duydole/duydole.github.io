Privacy Policy

Streaky: Habit Tracker · Effective October 18, 2026

In short: Streaky does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Information we collect

None. Streaky does not collect any personal information or usage data, and it does not send anything about you or your device to us or to third parties.

Information stored on your device

To work, Streaky keeps a few things only on your iPhone:

  • The habits you create (name, icon, color, daily goal, order and whether they are archived)
  • Your check-ins, that is, the days and times you logged each habit
  • Your reminder settings (time and days for each habit)
  • Whether you have finished the introduction screens, whether Streaky Pro is active, and a simple counter used to decide when to show Apple's rating prompt

This data is shared only between the Streaky app and its Home Screen widgets, through storage on your device. It never leaves your iPhone through Streaky. It is removed when you delete the app, and you can delete any habit and its history from the habit's detail screen. If you use iCloud or computer backups, Apple may include this data in your device backup under Apple's own privacy policy.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Streaky asks for notification permission only when you turn on a reminder, and nothing is sent through a server.

Exporting your data

With Streaky Pro you can export your habits as a CSV file. The file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to share or save it.

Purchases

Streaky Pro subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID, or payment details. Streaky only receives confirmation from Apple of whether your subscription is active.

Third-party services

Streaky has no third-party SDKs, advertising or analytics. Purchases and the rating prompt are provided by Apple (StoreKit). Links such as "Contact Support" open Apple's Mail app or Safari. These are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Streaky collects no personal information from anyone, so it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
