How do I get the best results?

Scroll so that each screenshot overlaps the previous one by at least a few lines of content, then select them in Stitchly (or tap Select Latest).

A seam looks wrong

Tap the seam label in the preview and nudge the lower screenshot up or down until the rows line up.
