Stitchly Support

How do I get the best results?

Scroll so that each screenshot overlaps the previous one by at least a few lines of content, then select them in Stitchly (or tap Select Latest).

A seam looks wrong

Tap the seam label in the preview and nudge the lower screenshot up or down until the rows line up.

Hiding private details

After stitching, Stitchly reads the text in your screenshots on your device and points out phone numbers, email addresses, street addresses, long card or account numbers, people's names, one-time codes, order numbers, licence plates, QR codes and faces. Before you save or share, it reminds you if anything is still showing. Tap Hide All in the banner, or tap the eye button to choose what to hide and whether to pixelate it or cover it with a black bar. Always double-check anything else you'd rather keep private. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy.

Stitching from the Share Sheet

In Photos (or any app), select the screenshots in top-to-bottom order, tap Share and choose Stitchly. Up to 12 screenshots can be stitched this way; open the app for longer stitches and seam tweaks.

Shortcuts, Siri and the Action Button

Say “Stitch my screenshots with Stitchly”, or add Stitch Latest Screenshots in the Shortcuts app. It stitches the screenshots you just took in a row and saves the result to Photos without opening the app. Assign that shortcut to the Action Button or Back Tap (Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap).
