Stitchly Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Stitchly does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Photos

With your permission, Stitchly reads screenshots from your photo library to stitch them into one image, and saves the result back to your library. All processing happens entirely on your device. Images are never uploaded anywhere.

Text in your screenshots

To find private details such as phone numbers, email addresses, street addresses and card or account numbers, Stitchly reads the text in your screenshots using Apple's on-device text recognition. This happens only on your device and only while you're stitching; the text is not saved or sent anywhere.

Share Sheet and Shortcuts

Images you share to Stitchly, or pass to its shortcuts, are processed on your device in the same way and saved to your photo library only when you choose to.

Analytics & tracking

Stitchly contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs and makes no network requests.
