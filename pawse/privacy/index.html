Privacy Policy

Pawse: Screen Time Pet · Effective October 21, 2026

In short: Pawse does not collect, upload, share or sell any personal data. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your app usage stays on your iPhone, inside Apple's Screen Time system.

Information we collect

None. Pawse has no servers and does not send anything about you or your device to us or to third parties.

Screen Time data

Pawse uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity) to block the apps and websites you choose.

  • The apps, categories and websites you pick are given to Pawse by iOS as opaque tokens. Pawse cannot see their names or turn them back into a list of your apps.
  • Usage charts (screen time, pickups and most-used apps) are drawn by an Apple-sandboxed report extension. That extension cannot send data anywhere, and the Pawse app itself cannot read these numbers.
  • Pawse only learns that a limit reached 50%, 90% or 100%, which it uses to change your pet's mood and warn you.

Information stored on your device

To work, Pawse keeps these only on your iPhone, shared between the app, its widgets and its Screen Time extensions through on-device storage:

  • Your pet (name, species, treats, XP, accessories)
  • Your block lists, schedules, daily limits and focus sessions
  • Daily progress such as focus minutes, completed sessions, quests and achievements
  • Your settings, such as breaks, the pet's voice and notification choices

This data is removed when you use Profile → Reset Pawse or delete the app. If you use iCloud or computer backups, Apple may include it in your device backup under Apple's own privacy policy.

Notifications

Reminders and alerts are local notifications created on your iPhone. Pawse asks for permission first, and nothing is sent through a server.

Third-party services

Pawse has no third-party SDKs, advertising or analytics. The rating prompt is provided by Apple. Links such as "Contact Support" open Apple's Mail app or Safari, which are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Pawse collects no personal information from anyone, so it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
