Common questions

Why does Pawse need Screen Time access?
Pawse uses Apple's Screen Time API to block the apps you choose. Apple keeps your app usage on your iPhone; Pawse never sees which apps you use or how long you use them.

How do I block apps?
Open Blocking, create a block list, then start a focus session, add a schedule (for example work hours or bedtime) or set a daily limit.

What is Deep Focus?
A focus session that can't be ended early and doesn't allow breaks. Gentle sessions can be ended early if you really need to.

How do breaks work?
When a blocked app opens, you can take a short break from the shield. Set how many breaks you get per day and how long they last in Profile → Breaks. Deep Focus and strict schedules never allow breaks.

Why is my pet sad?
Your pet's mood follows your habits: focus sessions and walking away from blocked apps make it happy; going over daily limits or ending sessions early makes it sad. Feed it with treats to cheer it up.

How do I remove every block?
Turn off the schedule or limit in Blocking, or use Profile → Reset Pawse, which removes your pet, rules and history and lifts every block. Deleting the app also removes all blocks.

Can I use Pawse in Vietnamese?
Yes. Pawse follows your iPhone language. To change only Pawse, open Settings → Pawse → Language.

Does Pawse cost anything?
No. Pawse is free, with no ads, no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.
