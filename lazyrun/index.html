Common questions

How does the daily goal grow?
You choose three things when you start: the distance for day 1, how much is added after each day you finish, and the longest daily goal. For example, 100 m on day 1 and +100 m per finished day, up to 5 km. Only finished days raise the goal.

Can I change my plan later?
Yes. Open Settings → Your Plan to change the first-day distance, the daily increase or the longest goal. Today's and future goals follow the new plan; days you already ran keep the goal they were run against.

What happens if I miss a day?
Every 7 days you finish in a row earns a Lazy Day (you can save up to 2). If you miss a day while you have one, it is used automatically and your streak lives on; the yellow sleepy dot on the week strip shows the day it covered. With no Lazy Day saved, the streak goes back to zero. Either way your level stays: the next goal picks up where you left off.

Do several short runs count?
Yes. All runs on the same day add up toward that day's goal.

The distance seems off.
Outdoors, LazyRun measures distance with GPS. It ignores readings that are too inaccurate and small wobbles while you stand still, so it works best with a clear view of the sky. Distance can lag at the start while GPS finds your position; the badge at the top of the run screen shows the signal.

Can I run indoors or on a treadmill?
Yes. When GPS is missing or weak for a few seconds, LazyRun switches to the iPhone's step counter and the badge shows Counting steps. Keep the phone on you (in a pocket or armband) and allow Motion & Fitness access when asked; you can turn it on later in the iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness → LazyRun. Step-based distance is an estimate and gets more accurate after you've done a few outdoor runs or walks with your iPhone.

Can I lock my phone during a run?
Yes. While a run is in progress, LazyRun keeps measuring with the screen locked, and iOS shows a location indicator. Tracking stops as soon as you pause or finish the run.

LazyRun says Location Is Off.
Open the iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → LazyRun and choose While Using the App.

Reminders don't appear.
Turn on Settings → Daily Reminder in LazyRun and pick a time, then make sure notifications are allowed in the iPhone Settings → Notifications → LazyRun. The reminder is skipped on days you've already finished.

How do I delete a run?
Open the History tab and swipe left on the run.

Does LazyRun cost anything?
No. LazyRun is free, with no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Will my runs sync to my other devices?
No. Your runs are stored only on this iPhone and are included in your regular iCloud or computer backup.
