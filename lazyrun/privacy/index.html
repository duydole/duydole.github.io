Privacy Policy

LazyRun: Tiny Daily Runs · Effective October 22, 2026

In short: LazyRun does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. Your location is used only on your iPhone, only during a run, to measure distance, and your route is never saved. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Information we collect

None. LazyRun does not collect any personal information or usage data, and it does not send anything about you or your device to us or to third parties.

Location

LazyRun asks for location access ("While Using the App") the first time you start a run. While a run is in progress, the app reads your position to add up the distance you cover, including while the screen is locked; iOS shows a location indicator during this time. Location updates stop when you pause or finish the run.

Positions are used only in memory to calculate distance. LazyRun does not save your route, coordinates or places, and location never leaves your iPhone.

Information stored on your device

To work, LazyRun keeps a few things only on your iPhone:

  • Your runs: when each run started, how long it lasted, the distance and that day's goal
  • Your plan: the first-day distance, the daily increase and the longest daily goal
  • Your reminder settings (on or off, and the time)
  • Whether you have finished the introduction screens, and a simple counter used to decide when to show Apple's rating prompt

This data never leaves your iPhone through LazyRun. It is removed when you delete the app, and you can delete any run from the History tab. If you use iCloud or computer backups, Apple may include this data in your device backup under Apple's own privacy policy.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. LazyRun asks for notification permission only when you turn on the daily reminder, and nothing is sent through a server.

Third-party services

LazyRun has no third-party SDKs, advertising or analytics. The rating prompt is provided by Apple (StoreKit). Links such as "Contact Support" and "Privacy Policy" open Apple's Mail app or Safari. These are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children's privacy

LazyRun collects no personal information from anyone, so it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
